Deauth

Normal mode targets a client detected on the AP. Optional broadcast is off by default and may disconnect every client on that AP.

Broadcast sends one deauth frame to the AP's broadcast address; it can interrupt all connected clients. Confirm each time before sending.

PMKID capture (no client, no deauth)

hcxdumptool style: sends reassociations from a made-up MAC and the AP answers with an EAPOL M1 that already carries the PMKID. Quieter than deauthing a real client. Works on WPA/WPA2 with roaming enabled.

Handshake capture (EAPOL)

Listens for the WPA/WPA2 4-way handshake and saves it to a .pcap. Lock the target AP channel and run a deauth in parallel to force the reconnect.
